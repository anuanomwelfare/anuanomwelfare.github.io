How This Portal Works

How do I log in?

Find your name in the list below, click it, and enter your 4-digit PIN.

I forgot my PIN — what now?

Click your name, then "Forgot PIN?" — you'll verify with your registered phone number (and a security question too, if you've set one up) before choosing a new PIN.

What's "Recovery Question" for?

An extra security step you can set up once you're logged in — it adds a second check before anyone can reset your PIN. Recommended, but optional.

Why is there an "Alternate Phone" option?

If you have two phone numbers on file, either one works when verifying your identity to reset your PIN.

Still stuck?

Reach out to your association's admin — they can look up your account and help directly.

Bulk Import Members

Upload a CSV file to add several members at once.

Format: one member per line — Name,Phone,PIN,AlternatePhone,JoinMonth (only Name is required; PIN defaults to 1234, JoinMonth defaults to July 2026). If a name contains a comma, wrap it in quotes, e.g. "Mensah, Kwame",0241112233,1234. JoinMonth matters — members only start owing dues from that month onward, so someone who joined later shouldn't be given an earlier date.
Building this in Excel? Format the phone column as Text before typing numbers in (Excel otherwise strips leading zeros), and use "Save As" → CSV — not .xlsx — before uploading here. Download a template
